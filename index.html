About Us

Our Journey to Excellence

We are a Kenya-based general supplies and construction firm driven by quality, trust, and results.

Founded in 2016

Cycon Invested Ltd started with a single truck, a small team, and a big vision — to offer dependable general supplies and construction services.

Through hard work, integrity, and consistent delivery, we've built a strong reputation for quality and reliability. Today, we are proud to be a trusted partner for homeowners, contractors, and institutions across Kenya.

With over 8 years of experience, Cycon continues to grow — driven by client trust, professionalism, and a commitment to excellence in every project.
